# FareScout 7-Day Promotion Campaign

Live demo: https://farescout.onrender.com

Campaign goal: position FareScout as a route-monitoring product, not just a one-time flight search demo. Every post should drive one action: drop a route, join the alert beta, or request the Founder Pilot.

## Core Message

```text
Stop checking the same flight route manually. FareScout searches live fares now and is preparing monitored price-drop alerts next.
```

Primary CTA:

```text
Drop your route and target price. I will scout the best ones with FareScout.
```

## Posting Checklist

- Use one CTA only.
- Ask for a route plus target price.
- Do not promise guaranteed savings.
- Do not say FareScout sells tickets.
- Reply to every comment within 24 hours.
- Put useful route examples in the first post or caption.
- After posting, check the private lead cockpit for source and campaign quality.

## Daily Operating Routine

```text
Morning:
- Post one primary channel asset.
- Reply to comments from the previous day.

Midday:
- Share to one secondary channel or friends group.
- Add best route suggestions to the private cockpit if they arrive outside the form.

Evening:
- Check leads by source/campaign.
- Mark lead status: new, contacted, interested, won, lost.
- Send reply playbook message to every serious lead.
```

## Success Criteria

```text
100+ visits from social links
20+ route comments or DMs
10+ beta or Founder Pilot leads
1-3 paid Founder Pilot attempts once Stripe Payment Link is connected
1-2 repeatable channels for route-alert leads
```
